Operating Systems are system software that manage computer hardware and software resources.

They provide common services for computer programs and act as an intermediary between users and the computer hardware.

Examples of operating systems include Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android.

In this subject, we will focus on installing and working with Windows OS.

Operating systems are essential for the functioning of computers and enable users to interact with their devices effectively.
